This study presents a longitudinal analysis of SARS-CoV-2 variant emergence, spread, and transmission dynamics across India from April 2020 through March 2021 — a period spanning the country's first and second pandemic waves and the rise of the Delta variant lineages. India, as one of the world's most populous nations, represented a critical epicenter for variant surveillance during this period, and understanding its internal transmission landscape had direct implications for global pandemic preparedness.

## Motivation

Genomic surveillance has been central to tracking SARS-CoV-2 evolution and the emergence of variants of concern (VOC). However, in populous and geographically diverse countries like India, understanding the regional dynamics of variant spread — which states drove early transmission, how lineages moved between regions, and at what rate the virus was diverging — required integrated longitudinal analysis of genome sequence data alongside geographic metadata.

## What We Built

- Analyzed SARS-CoV-2 whole-genome sequences deposited in **GISAID** from India spanning April 2020 to March 2021, covering all major pandemic waves during this period
- Characterized the **geographical landscape** of variant distribution across Indian states, tracking the rise and regional spread of B.1.617.2 (Delta), B.1.617.3, and other lineages of concern
- Traced the origin of B.1.617.3 to Maharashtra in October 2020, and B.1.617.2 to Bihar, with subsequent spread to Maharashtra, Gujarat, and West Bengal
- Estimated a **viral divergence rate** of 9.48 × 10⁻⁴ substitutions per site per year for the Indian SARS-CoV-2 dataset
- Built a real-time **transmission map** deployed at [covid19-indiana.soic.iupui.edu](https://covid19-indiana.soic.iupui.edu/India/EmergingLineages/April2020/to/March2021) to visualize variant emergence and spread across Indian states interactively

## Key Findings

- Delta (B.1.617.2) was first detected in Bihar before spreading to multiple major states, consistent with domestic origin and subsequent nationwide dissemination
- Regional analysis revealed heterogeneous variant landscapes across Indian states, with Maharashtra serving as a persistent hub for early variant circulation
- The divergence rate estimate aligned with global SARS-CoV-2 evolutionary rate estimates, supporting the robustness of the longitudinal dataset
