I'm Hunter Mathias Gill — a bioinformatician and AI/ML engineer based in Indianapolis, Indiana. I recently completed my Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Indiana University Indianapolis, with a Minor in Applied Data Science, and I'm currently working at Mercor. My research spans healthcare AI, medical imaging, ophthalmology, genomics, and bioactivity profiling, with a focus on building interpretable, objectively-grounded machine learning systems.

Outside of research and engineering, I spend most of my free time hiking and traveling with my family. We recently got back from the South Coast of Iceland.
